Artisan Lineage

A Commitment to Waxed Stitching

Leathervora was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of manual saddle-stitching and introduce it to luxury carry gear. In a world increasingly saturated by synthetic plastic nylon and weak machine lockstitches, we dedicate our studio to thick full-grain hides and hand-polished beeswax borders. We believe a travel duffle is not simply a container; it is a structural representation of strength, heritage, and tailor craft.

Each leather panel is sourced individually from certified tanneries in Tuscany. Once received at our New York workshop, the pieces are cut, burnished, and stitched entirely by hand. Organic tanning uses chestnut bark and mimosa leaves instead of toxic chromium chemicals, protecting your skin and the ecosystem. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our saddlers to sustain a Lifetime Restoration Guarantee.

Our Three Heritage Pillars

01. Tuscan Sourcing

We source every hide from local cooperatives in Tuscany, ensuring ethical labor and zero-carbon tanning.

02. Double Needle Lock

Bespoke stitch lines are locked twice. If a thread breaks, the line does not run or fall apart.

03. Cast Copper Fittings

We cast our metal buckles in solid copper, polishing each rivet in organic walnut oils.

The Saddlery Future

As Leathervora looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-craft principles. We maintain a small studio of certified saddlers, limiting our global output to a few hundred leather bags per year. This exclusivity ensures that every client registered receives the undivided focus and care of our master saddlers. Leathervora is a lifetime commitment to saddle stitch, organic beeswax, and heritage preservation.
